SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Storyboard & screen flow

A simple web application for managing clients, appointments and counselling records.

Navigation flow

The email domain assigns the account role at registration. Clients see their own dashboard, profile and appointments; counsellors access assigned management modules.

01Login / RegistrationRegister with an automatically assigned account role
02Role-specific dashboardClients and counsellors see separate workspaces
03Management modulesRole-specific access to appointments and records
